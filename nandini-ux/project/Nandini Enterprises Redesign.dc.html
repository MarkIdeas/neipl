About Nandini

Electrical, instrumentation and automation since 2006

An ISO 9001:2008 and CPRI certified company, Nandini Enterprises is a channel partner for Yaskawa, Honeywell, Baumer and Yokogawa. Sales engineers and service engineers work from one roof in Hyderabad and Pune, covering design, manufacturing, erection, commissioning and installation.

Vision

To be the most trusted progressive channel partner and leading service provider for electrical, instrumentation and automation needs in major projects that empower the nation.

Mission

To provide advanced, reliable electrical, instrumentation and control products with price, performance and service that meet and exceed expectations.
